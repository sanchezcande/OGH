01 · Brand Identity

Who we are

OpenGateHub is a small firm that does staff augmentation and hiring of senior developers. It is the only thing we do.

Tagline

Staff augmentation and hiring of senior developers.

English

Staff augmentation y contratación de developers senior.

Spanish

What the brand has to convey

A firm that chooses with care, where you talk to people. Not a platform, and not a generic agency.

  • SeriousSober design and plain claims. Nothing we can't back.
  • TrustworthyWe say what is free, what is paid and how we work.
  • ExclusiveSenior developers only. Few searches at a time.
  • HumanA small team. You talk to people.

Thesis

Whether a person fits the team is the first filter, not an extra.

We filter twice. First the technical side: does the developer master what the project needs. Then how they work: how they communicate, how they handle a problem, whether they understand the product. No developer reaches a client without passing both.

Founder

Candelaria Sanchez, Founder. She is a software engineer who started out in film. She founded OpenGateHub because she kept seeing the same problem everywhere: companies hiring great engineers who didn't fit the team. So she built a company where fitting the team is the first filter.

  • Her name is written Candelaria Sanchez, with no accent mark.
  • Her title is Founder. She is not the CTO. The CTO is Gustavo.
02 · Logo

Logo construction

Primary logo (wordmark)

The OpenGateHub logo is a typographic wordmark. There is no icon and no symbol: the name is the logo. It is set in Space Grotesk Bold with tight letter-spacing (-0.04em).

On ink

On paper

On the warm background

opengatehub

Construction rules

  • The wordmark is always lowercase: opengatehub
  • The syllable "gate" is always in the brand accent #CC5A50
  • "open" and "hub" stay in the base color: #111111 on light, white on dark
  • Font: Space Grotesk, weight 700
  • Letter-spacing: -0.04em
  • No icon, no period, no capitals, no modifications

Clear space

Keep a minimum clear space equal to the height of the letter "g" on all sides of the logo.

Minimum size

The logo is never rendered smaller than 18px (digital) or 12pt (print), so it stays legible.

Do
  • Use the wordmark as it is
  • Keep the accent on "gate"
  • Keep it lowercase, always
  • Place it on solid backgrounds
Don't
  • Capitalize any letter
  • Change the accent color
  • Add an icon, shadows or effects
  • Separate "open", "gate" and "hub"

05 · Visual Style

Composition

The layout is quiet: fine lines, straight buttons and a lot of air. It should read like a firm, not like a product page.

Lines, not cards

Blocks are separated by 1px lines, not by cards with shadows. A row of items is a top rule with thin dividers between columns.

  • We interview every developerYou only meet the ones who passed the interview.
  • Senior developers onlyFully remote.
  • You talk to peopleThere's no platform in between.

Border radius

Everything that has a corner uses 4px. The shapes are straight and sober, not rounded.

Buttons

Straight, 52px tall, ink on paper. Inter 600 at 15px, a 1px border and a 4px radius.

06 · Tone of Voice

How we speak

Brand personality

Direct, warm and sober. We sound like a smart colleague, not a brochure. Short sentences. No superlatives.

Principles

  • Company voice. On the site we speak as "we".
  • Clarity over cleverness. Simple words, short sentences, no jargon.
  • Honest over impressive. No superlatives and no figures we can't back.
  • Human over corporate. We talk like people, not like a press release.
Sounds like us
  • "We're a small team. You'll talk to people, not a platform."
  • "The decision is yours."
  • "That's why the filter goes before the hire, not after."
  • "No fine print."
Doesn't sound like us
  • "Leverage synergies"
  • "Cutting-edge solutions"
  • "World-class talent"
  • "We're passionate about innovation"

Language rules

Spanish

  • Rioplatense Spanish with voseo: "vos", "tenés", "agendá".
  • No em dash.
  • No opening marks (¿ ¡). A question carries only the closing one: "Cuánto sale y cómo se cobra?"
  • Never the English word "hire". We say "contratar" and "contratación".

English

  • Same register: plain, short and warm.
  • "Hire" and "hiring" are fine.

What we don't say

  • Anything about automation. OpenGateHub no longer offers it, and it does not sell custom software as a service either.
  • Any of the old figures: 9.7 satisfaction, 7.3 days, 87% on time, 50+ sprints, 300+ processes.
  • "Founder & CTO". The title is Founder.
  • Which region the developers are from. We say "100% remote" ("100% remoto").
  • "If it doesn't work out" ("si no funciona") anywhere outside the FAQ.
  • "You don't need to be technical" ("no hace falta que seas técnico"). That line belongs to the guide and the course, not to the service.
  • Clients, figures, awards or cases we can't back.
07 · Service

What we do

One service: staff augmentation and hiring of senior developers, 100% remote. There are no other service lines, and no piece should suggest there are.

Two ways to work

  • Staff augmentationThe developer joins your teamThey work as part of the client's team and OpenGateHub bills monthly.
  • HiringYou hire them directlyWe charge for the search and the client hires the developer directly.

Process

  1. 01
    The call

    20 minutes, free. The client tells us what they're building and what they need.

  2. 02
    We define what to look for

    First we define what the person has to prove. Only then do we start searching.

  3. 03
    We search and filter

    We look for senior developers, and each one goes through the technical filter and the human one.

  4. 04
    We present the best

    The client only meets the ones who passed both filters. The decision is theirs.

  5. 05
    They start working

    The developer joins the team and we bill monthly, or the client hires them directly and we charge for the search.

08 · Core Values

What we believe

Fit is the first filter

Whether a person fits the team is not an extra. We look at how each developer communicates, how they work, and whether they'll care about what they're building.

Two filters, in order

First, they know the craft. Then, everything else. No developer reaches a client without passing both parts. It's a standard we don't negotiate.

Few searches at a time

We're a small firm, and we like it that way. Taking on few searches is what lets us choose with care.

People, not a platform

Someone on our team talks to every candidate before presenting them. The client talks to people too. There's no platform in between.
