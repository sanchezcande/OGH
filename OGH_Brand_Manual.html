01 — Brand Identity

Who we are

OpenGateHub is a software and automation company from Argentina that helps businesses eliminate manual work through workflow automation, AI integration, and embedded nearshore engineering teams.

Tagline

"Workflow automation & staff augmentation for teams that move fast."

Mission

We believe in doing meaningful work with people who care. Our mission is to support founders and teams who want to create value with integrity, for their users, their investors, and the world they're part of.

Vision

We build technology by putting people first — because strong products start with strong teams. We're not another dev agency. We form long-term partnerships with teams that value cultural fit as much as technical excellence.

Origin Story

OpenGateHub was born from a simple idea: that technology should make life easier — and more meaningful. What started in Argentina has grown into a global team working with people who care about how things are built, not just what gets built.

Founder

Candelaria Sanchez — Founder & CTO. Software engineer, filmmaker turned founder, based in Bali. She started OpenGateHub because she kept seeing the same problem everywhere: companies hiring great engineers who just didn't fit the team. Smart people in the wrong context, producing friction instead of flow. So she built a company where cultural fit isn't a nice-to-have — it's the first filter.

02 — Logo

Logo Construction

Primary Logo (Wordmark)

The OpenGateHub logo is a pure typographic wordmark. There is no icon or symbol — the brand name is the logo. It's set in Space Grotesk Bold with tight letter-spacing (-0.04em).

Dark background

opengatehub

Light background

opengatehub

Construction Rules

  • The wordmark is always lowercase: opengatehub
  • The word "gate" is always in the accent color #CC5A50
  • "open" and "hub" remain in the base color (white on dark, #111 on light)
  • Font: Space Grotesk, weight 700
  • Letter-spacing: -0.04em
  • No period, no capitalization, no modifications

Clear Space

Maintain a minimum clear space equal to the height of the letter "g" on all sides of the logo.

Minimum Size

The logo should never be rendered smaller than 18px (digital) or 12pt (print) to ensure legibility.

DO
  • Use the wordmark as-is
  • Maintain the accent on "gate"
  • Keep it lowercase always
  • Use on solid backgrounds
DON'T
  • Capitalize any letters
  • Change the accent color
  • Add shadows or effects
  • Separate "open", "gate", "hub"

06 — Tone of Voice

How we speak

Brand Personality

Direct, warm, and grounded. We sound like a smart colleague — not a corporate brochure. We're data-driven but human. We say what we mean, and we don't oversell.

Principles

  • Clarity over cleverness — Simple words, short sentences, no jargon
  • Outcomes over features — Talk about what changes, not what we built
  • Honest over impressive — No inflated metrics, no buzzwords
  • Human over corporate — We talk like people, not press releases
USE
  • "We help you take that time back"
  • "No pitch, just a clear next step"
  • "We build with clarity, intention, and care"
  • "Cultural fit is the first filter"
AVOID
  • "Leverage synergies"
  • "Cutting-edge solutions"
  • "Seamless, robust, scalable"
  • "We're passionate about innovation"

Key Messages

  • "Your team loses 8+ hours/week to tasks automation handles in minutes."
  • "We're not another dev agency. We form long-term partnerships."
  • "Being human-first means reducing friction, not adding process."
  • "We don't chase trends — we build what matters."
  • "Every week you wait, it costs you."
07 — Services

What we do

1. Workflow Automation

We help companies remove manual work, connect systems, and standardize operations. We design, build, and deploy automations that reduce errors, accelerate execution, and free teams for higher-value work.

Process: Workflow Audit → Automation Blueprint → Build & Integrate → Deploy & Enable → Optimize & Scale

2. Staff Augmentation

We scale teams prioritizing cultural fit and human quality. We don't just find expert developers — we find people who align with your values, your way of working, and your vision. We work as part of your team to deliver quality results and accelerate your roadmap.

Process: Understand Culture → Select for Fit → Quick Integration → Work Together

Engagement Model

  1. Operational Discovery: Map current process, bottlenecks, and priorities
  2. Execution Plan: Define the right model (Automation, Staff Aug, or both)
  3. Launch & Optimize: Implement, measure impact, and improve continuously

Technologies

React, Next.js, Node.js, Prisma, Tailwind CSS, n8n, Make, AWS, Firebase, Stripe, and more.
